## Slide 7 — Responsible AI & Privacy
- AI cannot predict future sales with complete accuracy
- Forecasts contain uncertainty and assumptions
- Human verification is required
- Do not share names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, or payment credentials with AI tools without approved safeguards

## Slide 8 — Recommendations & Conclusion
1. Protect stock availability for high-contribution products
2. Plan around strong sales periods
3. Target regions differently
4. Use revenue + unit volume together
5. Track recurring KPIs

**Conclusion:** AI is useful for analysis and ideation, but outputs should remain evidence-based, privacy-aware, and human-verified.
